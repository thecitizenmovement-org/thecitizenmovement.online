Critical requirements

A redaction that can be undone is not a redaction. Each requirement below is a release gate.

  • Redaction permanently removes the underlying text.
  • Hidden text and applicable metadata are removed.
  • OCR layers and embedded content are checked.
  • Recovery through copy and paste is prevented.
  • Original evidence is preserved separately, untouched.
  • A redaction audit record is maintained.
  • Review is required before any external distribution.
