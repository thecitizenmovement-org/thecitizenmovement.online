What this page cannot do yet

Backend is hosted on Render (free tier). The backend lives at scam-intel-backend.onrender.com. The free tier sleeps after 15 minutes idle, so the first scan after a pause can take about a minute to wake up — signing in pings it awake. If a scan says the backend is unreachable, wait a minute and try again.

Scan history needs Phase 2 auth. Members sign in through the citizen-members project, but the scan tables live in the separate scam-intel project, whose row-level security only accepts that project's own session tokens. Until Phase 2 wires Supabase JWT auth into the backend (or the projects share an auth setup), your history may come back empty here even though scans were stored. The backend still records every scan it runs.

No quotas or payments yet. Stripe gating and usage quotas arrive in Phase 3.
