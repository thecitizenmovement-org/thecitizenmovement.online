Use your member email and the password you already use. The sign-in page never fills in your email for you, and it never saves your password. If sign-in fails, tell Josh — do not set a new password on your own.

How do I edit my page?

Talk to your Muse first. Tell it in plain words what you want to change. It has read your papers, so it knows what you are talking about. It will help you write the draft. Then save the draft in the Editing Desk, and mark it ready to publish when it says what you mean. One step at a time. There is no rush.

What can my Muse do for me?

  • Tell you plainly which side of the line something falls on — Josh’s domain, or Shasta County Wars.
